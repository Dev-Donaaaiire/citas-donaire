# META2027 - Elite Frontend Standards

## Inviolable UI/UX Engineering Laws
1. **NO GENERIC AI GRADIENTS**: Cliché purple-to-blue / neon violet gradients are strictly forbidden. Monochromatic zinc tones only with surgical single-color accent highlights.
2. **Typography**: Modern grotesk typography (`Geist`, `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`) with `tracking-tight` on headlines and generous spacing.
3. **Surfaces & Borders**: Subtle hairline borders (`border-white/10` in dark, `border-zinc-200/80` in light), soft multilayer shadows, `backdrop-blur-md` surfaces.
4. **Tactile Micro-Interactions**: Tactile active states (`active:scale-[0.98]`), hover transitions, and Framer Motion spring physics.
5. **Mobile-First (Instagram conversion)**: `100dvh` viewport adaptation, generous thumb touch targets (≥ 48px), and sticky bottom conversion actions.
